## 1. Photography (the reshoot)

### Technical spec — give this to whoever shoots

- **Resolution:** minimum 2000 px on the long edge; 3000 px ideal. Shoot RAW if possible so we can grade consistently.
- **Format for the site:** JPEG, 80–90% quality, exported at ~1800 px long edge.
- **Light:** natural window light or warm consistent light. Avoid mixed lighting (daylight + tungsten in the same frame) — it's what makes the current photos unusable.
- **Orientation:** shoot every scene in **both landscape and portrait** so we have crops for desktop and mobile.
- **Palette:** the site is sand / terracotta / olive — wooden boards, linen, brass, ceramic and greenery photograph beautifully against it; avoid blue-heavy props.

### Worth shooting beyond the current slots (for future sections)

- **Hands at work** — plating, tearing bread, spice grinding. Motion and shallow depth; these become section backgrounds and future site content.
- **Ingredient / process textures** — preserved lemons in jars, ras el hanout, dough, charcoal grill. The "cured in-house" claims on the site deserve proof.
- **Service moments** — plates leaving the pass, the crew working, a sauce being poured at the table. These sell events better than any plated dish.
- **Venue/room establishing shots** — the kinds of rooms he cooks in (private dining room, marquee, boardroom).
- **Optional: 10–20 s video clips** of plating or pouring — a muted hero loop is a strong future upgrade.

## 2. Words & facts (deepening the story)

The narrative is right — these details make it specific. Questions for Alan:

1. Where does the Moroccan side come from — family, a city, a grandmother's kitchen? One concrete detail ("my mother's Friday couscous in Fès") beats a paragraph of adjectives.
2. Which London kitchens/restaurants did he train or work in? Named venues or chefs are instant credibility.
3. How many years cooking professionally?
4. What is *the* signature dish — the one he wants to be known for?
5. Sourcing story — markets, named suppliers, spices imported from Morocco?
6. How does a booking actually work — enquiry → consultation → tasting → menu → event? Lead times, deposit, travel radius?
7. How big is the crew, and who are they?
